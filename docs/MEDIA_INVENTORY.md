## Replacing a placeholder

1. Capture a genuine Unreal Editor screenshot or GIF for the listed example. Diagrams may be newly authored if they accurately represent the implementation.
2. Save the final asset at `docs/assets/media/<asset name>`, using the listed `.png` or `.gif` extension.
3. Change the corresponding figure's `<img src>` to its actual relative path; leave the `data-media-id`, `data-kind`, and original reader-facing caption in place unless the final media warrants a factual correction.
4. Confirm media stays within the article width, has useful alternative text, and is readable at common desktop and mobile sizes.
5. Update [media-slots.json](assets/media-slots.json) if asset scope, type, caption, or instructions change.
